An original cover for Grand Theft Auto VI: its two leads, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos — drawn to their official character designs, Jason in a pale tank top with chain necklace and arm tattoo, Lucia in a pink long-sleeve crop top and camo leggings with her dark curls in a high ponytail — stand in the foreground looking out over a neon Vice City beach strip at sunset: art-deco hotels in pink and teal, a pastel-pink convertible, flamingos in the shallows and a helicopter over the water, under the game's name in plain block letters.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

The program copies `entry.png` (720×960 PNG, 1,001,422 bytes) to the output directory and verifies the copy is complete; it writes the same bytes on every run.

## Design note

One scene, read at a glance: the pair in the foreground, the city they are about to own spread out in front of them. The composition is original — a wide vista rather than the official cover's panel collage — and every classic ingredient is drawn fresh for this cover: the sunset palette, the neon deco strip, the palms, the convertible, the flamingos. The leads' faces, hair and outfits follow Rockstar's official character art for Jason and Lucia. The title is set in plain block capitals, no logo artwork.
