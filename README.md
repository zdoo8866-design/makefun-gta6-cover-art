An original cover for Grand Theft Auto VI: its two leads, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, stand in the foreground with their backs to the viewer, rendered as dark silhouettes rim-lit by the sunset — Jason broad and muscular in a sleeveless tank top, his bare arms outlined in neon teal; Lucia beside him, her curly hair in its signature high ponytail, long-sleeve crop top and camo pants, one hand on her hip — looking out over a neon Vice City beach strip at sunset: art-deco hotels in pink and teal, a pastel-pink convertible, flamingos in the shallows and a helicopter over the water, under the game's name in plain block letters.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

The program copies `entry.png` (720×960 PNG, 1,015,479 bytes) to the output directory and verifies the copy is complete; it writes the same bytes on every run.

## Design note

One scene, read at a glance: the pair in the foreground, the city they are about to own spread out in front of them. The composition is original — a wide vista rather than the official cover's panel collage — and every classic ingredient is drawn fresh for this cover: the sunset palette, the neon deco strip, the palms, the convertible, the flamingos. The leads never show their faces: they are pure back-view silhouettes, recognizable from the outlines of Rockstar's official character designs — Jason's build and tank top, Lucia's high ponytail and camo pants — with every detail left in shadow. The title is set in plain block capitals, no logo artwork.
